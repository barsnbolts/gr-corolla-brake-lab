V2A · arm attachment

One uninterrupted open spoon carries two small source-guided arm contact lands and two integral strap slots. r1 lifts its outboard floor so the air leaves rising toward the rotor instead of flat into the dust shield. Strap selection, liner take-up and retention under load remain to be resolved.

V2B · body attachment

One broad apron flows into a curved wall with two integral mounting ears on the captured wheel-arch liner, placed so each fastener can be reached. The scan does not capture the undertray there, so the seats, fixings and support strength remain inferred.

Saved geometry and motion

Each saved model is one sound solid and passes a meaningful edit and reopening. Local scan crossings, 19 sampled steering positions, release for removal and straight tool access to every fixing are clear. Exact scope is recorded in the review.

These checks do not establish continuous clearance, suspension travel or the complete wheel envelope.

Air path and obstruction

At the approximate straight-ahead pose, 10 of 15 sampled exit lines from each guide now reach rotor metal first (previously 2 for V2A and 6 for V2B). These geometric lines do not prove flow or cooling, and the check does not steer the knuckle; exact results are in the review.

Before fabrication

Resolve the real fixing and backing stack, strap material and temperature rating, loaded retention, service access and complete moving envelopes. These digital models are editable packaging proposals. V1 stays closed; V3/V4 remain research and later work.

ONE-PIECE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

A smooth guide with simple mounting.

Continuous turning surface

Keep the pickup and turn in one smooth, open surface. Adjust width, sweep and turning radius around the arm, shield and moving brake parts.

Integral mounting features

Keep the arm lands and strap slots within V2A, and the panel mounting ears within V2B. Review access, drainage, debris exposure, heat and support before fabrication.
