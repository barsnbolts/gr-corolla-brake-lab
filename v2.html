What is checked so far?

Geometry and editability

Both candidates are valid single solids. Meaningful parameter changes pass export and reopening. Local captured-scan checks found no transverse crossings.

Nominal steering

Both separate from the four tested brake-source regions at 19 sampled steering poses. One scoop/shield hull overlap was resolved against dense triangles. Arm and body stay at their captured suspension position; suspension travel and wheel clearance remain unverified.
