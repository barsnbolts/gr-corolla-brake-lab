02 · Caliper/knuckle scoop

Problem: The faceted tray has no credible installed pickup, support and discharge layout. The inspected arm-mounted example does not establish the requested mount.

Direction: Recover the exact caliper/knuckle donor and define its mounting and turning surface. Do not simply bolt a bracket onto this tray.

03 · Hose-fed rotor cooling

Problem: A generic adapter and blank flange do not define the rotor-vane interface or attachment.

Direction: Choose between a shaped backing-plate mechanism and carrier integration using actual assembly evidence. The hose solid can be omitted; its approach and movement cannot.

04 · Side entry is mandatory

A different architecture, not a rotated neck.

The proposed direction is a low distribution shell following the caliper, inspired by installed motorsport ducting. A side approach must avoid hydraulic lines, fittings, the wheel and strut, while allowing pad service. The current twin-rail form is not selected.

A shared supply does not guarantee a useful flow split. Caliper washing does not prove direct pad cooling. Those decisions remain open.

Review before modelling

The next work, in order.

  1. Reference-led layouts: Show each inlet, target, attachment, moving group and service path in comparable, uncropped views.
  2. Assembly frame: Reconcile rotor axis, scan landmarks and photographs. Distinguish apparent vertical from measured gravity and camber; establish scale before physical tolerances.
  3. Architecture review: Review those layouts before restarting geometry. Build a simple installed envelope first, then compare it directly with the donor.
  4. Validation: Check attachment, target and obstruction, service and motion, then detailed mesh and saved-file integrity. Cooling performance needs separate evidence.

Useful reuse

Protected scan, photographs, identified landmarks, reference mechanisms, inspection views and checking tools. Options 3 and 4 may share the rotor circuit after it is selected.

Still unresolved

Physical scale and orientation calibration, hidden mount axes, wheel and motion envelopes, the exact option 2 donor, and final air targets. None of the rejected shapes is retained by default.
