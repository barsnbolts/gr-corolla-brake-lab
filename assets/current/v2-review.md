The existing34-reference review and six GPT-6.1 High dimensional reviews were integrated before the new modelling. A further GPT-6.1 High comparison considered six architectures: broad arm saddle, low arm twin vanes, body-fixed forked ramp, liner-fed open cheek, steering-following backing-plate guide, and mechanically disconnected body-to-arm relay. All remain concepts except the current two models.

Next mainline comparison: **low arm saddle with two broad turning vanes versus one continuous spoon** using the same source-guided retention; then **body-fixed forked ramp versus uninterrupted ramp** at the same pickup envelope.
