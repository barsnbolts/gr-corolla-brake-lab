# V2 review: arm scoop and body shroud, r1 (23 October 2026)

Two equal one-piece options. **Arm scoop (V2A)** straps to the lower arm. **Body shroud (V2B)** fixes to the formed wheel-arch liner. Each is one sound, editable solid. Neither establishes installed fit, full suspension travel, retention strength, heat behaviour or cooling performance. V1 stays closed; V3/V4 come next.

## What changed in r1

**Arm scoop.** The accepted scoop sent its discharge flat outboard, (forward, outboard, up) ≈ (0.0, 1.0, 0.1), straight into the dust shield. r1 lifts the outboard floor edge 8 source units (−166 → −158). The discharge now rises 7–42° across the exit, still outboard along the arm as on the PIRAS kit, toward the annulus inside the shield's inner edge where the rotor vanes draw air. Nothing else changed: same lands, strap slots and envelope.

**Body shroud.** The accepted shroud's discharge was yawed forward, so most sightlines hit the caliper body. r1 turns the last apron segment 5° rearward and 6 up, so the discharge leaves straight outboard at about 35° up. Two mounting fixes came from a new access check:

- each ear's support beam used to land right over its fixing hole, blocking the fastener head. It now lands on the shoe's other end;
- ear 1's seat had its fastener head facing chassis structure about 12 mm away. It moved 24 mm to the inboard face of the same liner flap, which has 150 mm of clear approach.

The scan does not capture the undertray at the shroud: there are no scan points in the 30 mm above its floor. So the A'PEXi-style undertray clip holes can't be located, and both ears stay **inferred** seats on the captured liner.
